Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — transparently.

Athia is built on the principle of user sovereignty. That extends to how we handle your data on this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What We Collect

This website uses Microsoft Clarity for free, anonymous analytics. Clarity helps us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Clarity collects:

  • Page views — which pages you visit and how long you stay.
  • Scroll depth — how far down a page you scroll.
  • Clicks and interactions — where you click on the page.
  • Device information — browser type, screen size, and general device category.
  • General location — country-level location derived from your IP address.

Clarity does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, etc.).
  • Content you type into forms or inputs.
  • Sensitive data or passwords.
  • Information that can be used to identify you personally.

How We Use the Data

We use the aggregated, anonymous data from Clarity to:

  • Understand which parts of the website are most useful.
  • Identify pages that need improvement.
  • Optimize the site for different devices and browsers.

We do not use analytics data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond improving this website.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Opt out of Clarity tracking at any time.
  • Request deletion of any data that may be associated with you.
  • Access information about what data is stored.

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of Microsoft Clarity by installing the Clarity Opt-Out browser extension. Once installed, Clarity will not collect data from your browser.

Alternatively, you can clear your browser cookies or use your browser's privacy settings to block tracking.

Cookies

Microsoft Clarity sets a cookie to identify your browser session and to honor opt-out requests. The cookie does not contain personal information and is used solely for analytics purposes.

Data Storage

Clarity data is processed and stored by Microsoft in accordance with their privacy statement. Data is retained for up to 10 years unless deleted earlier.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and note the date of the change at the top. Significant changes may also be announced in our project documentation.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our repository or reach out through our community channels.

Your privacy matters to us.

We believe an operating system that respects user sovereignty should start with its own website.
