Community

Athia is built by the community, for the community. Join in on the fun.

Our Philosophy

Athia is community-driven. No single entity owns or controls it. Governance is transparent, inclusive, and accountable to the people who build and use it.

If you care about user sovereignty, security, and transparency, we want you to join us.
