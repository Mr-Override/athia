About Athia

The origin, vision, and founding principles of an independent operating system.

Origin & Vision

Computers are personal. They hold our memories, our work, and our creative output — yet the people who use them have had diminishing say over how they work.

Athia exists to reverse this. We're building an OS from scratch with modern tools, modern principles, and a modern understanding of what users need.

✓

What Athia Is

  • Independent, open-source OS
  • Built from scratch in Rust
  • User sovereignty first
  • Community-governed
  • Own microkernel & desktop
✕

What It's Not

  • Not a Linux distro
  • Not Linux-based
  • Not a fork or customization
  • Not proprietary
  • Not cloud-dependent
