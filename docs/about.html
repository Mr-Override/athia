About Athia

The origin, vision, and founding principles of an independent operating system.

Origin & Vision

Computers are personal. They hold our memories, our work, our communications, and our creative output. And yet, for too long, the people who use these machines have had diminishing say over how they work.

Athia exists to reverse this trend. We are building an operating system from the ground up — not by forking, customizing, or extending an existing one, but by starting fresh with modern tools, modern principles, and a modern understanding of what users need.

What Athia Is

  • A completely independent, open-source operating system
  • Built from the ground up, primarily in Rust
  • Designed around user sovereignty, security, and transparency
  • Community-driven and transparently governed
  • A complete OS with its own microkernel, services, desktop, and application framework

What Athia Is Not

  • A Linux distribution
  • A Linux-based operating system
  • A customized version of an existing operating system
  • A proprietary product
  • A cloud-dependent operating system

Founding Principles

  1. User sovereignty — You own and control your computer.
  2. Security first — Security is a foundational architectural requirement.
  3. Open source — Development, documentation, and decision-making are transparent.
  4. Offline first — The OS remains functional without internet connectivity.
  5. User-controlled updates — Updates are never silently forced upon users.
  6. Privacy — No mandatory accounts, telemetry, or cloud dependency.
  7. Extensibility — Users have extensive control over their operating environment.
  8. Longevity — Users can maintain their systems over time.
  9. Community ownership — Athia is governed transparently and developed collaboratively.

Long-Term Ambition

Athia aims to create a complete, polished, secure, customizable, and fully usable operating system. The bar is high, and we intend to meet it. This is not a hobby project — it is a serious undertaking to build an operating system that puts users first.

Built for the people who use it. Owned by the people who build it. Controlled by the people who run it.

Current Status

Athia is in the pre-development documentation phase. The repository contains architectural documentation, design documents, and project scaffolding. No functional operating system code has been implemented yet.

Athia — "Your machine. Your rules."
